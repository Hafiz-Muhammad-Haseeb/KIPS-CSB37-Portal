KIPS CSB-37 Nexus

KIPS CSB-37 Nexus is a class management portal designed to bring essential class administration workflows together in one organized place. It supports the CSB-37 community with a central point for academic coordination and class records.

Attendance & Records

Organize class attendance information and related student records.

Leave Applications

Provide a structured workflow for submitting and reviewing leave applications.

Fine Management

Keep fine entries, payment status and related records organized.

Announcements

Share class updates, schedules and important announcements in one place.

Access to student and administrative records is intended for authorized portal users.
