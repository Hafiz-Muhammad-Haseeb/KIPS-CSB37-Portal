CSB-37 Rules & Leave Policy

Official KIPS College disciplinary fine policy, attendance regulations, leave procedures, and clearance deadlines.

TEST ATTENDANCE POLICY

Absent / Leave During Test

Fine Amount: PKR 200

Unexcused absence or submitting leave on a scheduled class test day incurs a mandatory PKR 200 fine.

STUDY DAYS POLICY

Absent During Study Days

Fine Amount: PKR 150

Missing preparation days during study schedules results in a PKR 150 fine per missed day.

SATURDAY ATTENDANCE

Absent / Leave on Saturday

Strict Penalty: PKR 500

Saturday classes are mandatory. Being absent or taking leave on Saturday carries a PKR 500 fine.

LEAVE LIMIT POLICY

Monthly Study Days Leave Limit

Max Allowed: Only 2 Leaves / Month

A maximum of only 2 leaves are permitted during study days per month across the session with prior approval (excluding test days).

MARRIAGE LEAVE

Marriage Leave Guidelines

Exemption: Up to 3 Leaves

Marriage leave grants 3 allowed leaves (even on test or normal days) provided the wedding card and proof are submitted in advance.

CLEARANCE DEADLINE

Dues Clearance Deadline

Timeline: Within 3 Days

All pending fines must be cleared within 3 days of issuance or reported through the Dispute Appeals tab.

PRIVACY · SECURITY · DATA INTEGRITY

NEXUS DATA PROTECTION PRINCIPLE

Your data is protected through the security controls implemented by the portal. Sensitive information is handled according to access permissions and applicable data-protection practices.

01. Account Security & Authentication

Portal access is restricted through registered account credentials and server-side authentication. Users must keep their passwords confidential and must not share their accounts with unauthorized individuals.

02. Data Confidentiality

Student profiles, fine records, payment information, leave applications and administrative records are intended for authorized portal use. Access is governed by the permissions assigned to each account.

03. Encryption & Secure Transmission

Where encryption is configured, protected information is encrypted during storage or transmission using the applicable security mechanisms. Secure HTTPS transmission must be enabled in production.

04. Audit Trail & Data Integrity

Important administrative activities, fine updates, payment entries and review decisions may be recorded in the server-side audit history to support accountability and record integrity.

05. Authorized Use & Third-Party Access

Users must not attempt unauthorized access, manipulate records, extract confidential information or share private student data with unauthorized parties.

06. Fine Records & Appeals

Students are responsible for reviewing their fine records and submitting legitimate leave applications or disputes through the official portal procedures. Administrative decisions are subject to the applicable class policy.

07. Responsible Account Usage

Users must provide accurate information, protect their login credentials and immediately report suspected unauthorized account activity to the administrator.

SECURITY COMMITMENT

CSB-37 Nexus is designed to support controlled access, responsible information handling and reliable administrative record management.
