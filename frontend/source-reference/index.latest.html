CSB-37 Rules & Leave Policy

Official KIPS College disciplinary fine policy, attendance regulations, leave procedures, and clearance deadlines.

TEST ATTENDANCE POLICY

Absent / Leave During Test

Fine Amount: PKR 200

Unexcused absence or submitting leave on a scheduled class test day incurs a mandatory PKR 200 fine.

STUDY DAYS POLICY

Absent During Study Days

Fine Amount: PKR 150

Missing preparation days during study schedules results in a PKR 150 fine per missed day.

SATURDAY ATTENDANCE

Absent / Leave on Saturday

Strict Penalty: PKR 500

Saturday classes are mandatory. Being absent or taking leave on Saturday carries a PKR 500 fine.

LEAVE LIMIT POLICY

Monthly Study Days Leave Limit

Max Allowed: Only 2 Leaves / Month

A maximum of only 2 leaves are permitted during study days per month across the session with prior approval (excluding test days).

MARRIAGE LEAVE

Marriage Leave Guidelines

Exemption: Up to 3 Leaves

Marriage leave grants 3 allowed leaves (even on test or normal days) provided the wedding card and proof are submitted in advance.

CLEARANCE DEADLINE

Dues Clearance Deadline

Timeline: Within 3 Days

All pending fines must be cleared within 3 days of issuance or reported through the Dispute Appeals tab.
